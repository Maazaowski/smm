Welcome to **maazaowski**, my corner of the internet where I share stories, insights, and hot takes from life as a software & AI engineer.

## Why This Blog?

I've been in the industry for a while now, and I've realized that the best way to grow is to share what you learn. This blog is my attempt at that. Raw, unfiltered thoughts about:

- **AI Engineering**: building agents, LLM patterns, what works and what doesn't
- **Web Development**: the ever-evolving frontend landscape
- **Career**: navigating the tech industry, interviews, leadership
- **Tech News**: my reactions to what's happening in the world of tech
